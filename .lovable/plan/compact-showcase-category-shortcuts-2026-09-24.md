# Compact showcase category shortcuts

## What will change
- Remove the large duplicated brochure photo from the Immersive showcase header.
- Add one compact, horizontally scrollable row of gold-accented shortcuts for Brochure, Videos, Products, Packages, and Testimonies.
- Show shortcuts only for sections that contain visible content.
- Tapping a shortcut will smoothly move the existing showcase panel to that section while preserving the shared search, carousels, captions, buttons, and media.

## Technical details
- Update only the Immersive showcase presentation.
- Reuse the existing section anchors and design-system controls.
- Keep all card and shared-library content unchanged.
- Verify the result in mobile and desktop views.
